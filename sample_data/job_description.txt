Data Analyst

About the role
We are looking for a data analyst to turn product and customer data into clear recommendations for business teams.

Responsibilities
- Query, clean, and analyze datasets using SQL and Python.
- Build dashboards and reports with Power BI or Tableau.
- Use Pandas, statistics, and data visualization to identify trends.
- Explain findings to stakeholders and collaborate across teams.
- Present measurable recommendations based on reliable analysis.

Qualifications
- Bachelor degree in statistics, computer science, economics, or a related field.
- At least 2 years of experience in analytics or business intelligence.
- Experience with SQL, Python, Excel, and a business intelligence tool.
- Strong communication and problem-solving skills.
